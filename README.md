## 🛡️ Summary of Data & Privacy Practices

- **Zero Personal Data Collection**: RateSync does not require user accounts, logins, or collection of personal identifiers (no names, emails, addresses, or phone numbers).
- **100% On-Device Storage**: User preferences, currency watchlists, calculator inputs, rate alerts, and widget customizations are stored locally on-device.
- **No Financial Credential Storage**: All in-app purchases and subscriptions are securely processed through Google Play Billing. RateSync never accesses or stores credit card numbers or banking information.
- **Anonymous Market Feeds**: Currency rates and charts are fetched anonymously from open providers and official European Central Bank (ECB) data sources.
- **Diagnostics**: Anonymized crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose app stability without tracking users.
